What the correction changes

The corrected Greedy (src/methods/greedy_corrected.py, runs labelled GREEDY_CORR) keeps every decision rule of the submitted Greedy — stop only when forced, charge to full when stopping, 95%-quantile one-step guard, 10% battery buffer, queue avoidance — but executes each stop exactly as the MILP does:

  • C1 A charge before a daily rest is sequential (Eq. 14): the 5-min repositioning is paid and the charge is working time.
  • C2 A charge is credited as break only if it lasts at least the break (Eq. 15); otherwise the full break follows the charge, with the repositioning. The truck no longer idles at the bay.
  • C3 No charge shorter than the 15-min minimum (Eq. 9).
  • D1 At a mandatory break, a charge is added only if it is free (covers the break) or forced by the energy state.
  • D2 The spread rules use the exact dwell the simulator executes (incl. repositioning and access time).
