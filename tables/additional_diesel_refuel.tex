% one-sentence footnote for the diesel section
The diesel schedules charge no refuelling time.  Route lengths are medium 1541--2536\,km ($n=52$), against a tank range of 1636--3240\,km, so no fuel stop is required on the short routes under any specification and at most one on the medium routes.  Crediting the diesel with that stop (19\,min: access manoeuvre, queue and pumping) would change the reported penalty by -0.64 to +0.00\,percentage points, so the tables assume none --- the conservative direction.
