\begin{abstract}
Rough beats, one sentence each in final version:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Battery-electric trucks: charging and regulated driver breaks/rests
        (EU HoS) must be scheduled jointly under stochastic travel times;
        breaks can be taken \emph{inside} charging dwells, coupling the two.
  \item Benchmark finding (companion paper): rolling-horizon lookahead with an
        \emph{exact (MIP) tail} halves the gap to the hindsight optimum vs.\ the
        LP-relaxed tail (e.g.\ 4.3\%$\to$2.1\%, 7.6\%$\to$2.9\% short routes;
        4.6\%$\to$2.4\%, 6.8\%$\to$2.2\% medium; \todo{long-route numbers —
        recompile additional\_la\_stats with new runs}).
  \item But: median per-stop decision time 45--89\,s (route-class medians),
        worst stops 3--5\,min, 0.6--4.1\,h wall clock per route
        $\Rightarrow$ a \emph{batch} policy, not an operational one.
  \item Contribution: distill the exact-tail policy into a small
        feasibility-masked network deciding in $\sim$1\,ms; execute in closed
        loop in the same simulator; certify against BOTH the teacher and the
        hindsight oracle, on the full instance grid.
  \item OOD: train on subsets of route lengths / TW classes, deploy on held-out
        ones; teacher numbers exist everywhere, so the imitation gap is
        measured in- and out-of-distribution.
  \item Headline result: \todo{student gap X\% vs teacher Y\% vs LP-tail Z\%, at
        $10^4\times$ lower decision latency}.
\end{itemize}
\end{abstract}

\section{Method: distilling the exact-tail policy}\label{sec:method}
\begin{itemize}
  \item MDP view (2--3 sentences): state $s_i$ at stop $i$ = (SoC $e$, clocks
        $cd, sd, sw$, split flag $\phi$, rest budget $\rho_2$, ext-shift use,
        arrival time $t$) + instance context (next-$K$ stops: leg lengths,
        charger power, TW offsets relative to $t$; aggregates of remaining
        route).  Action = (charge $y$, break type $\in\{$none,15,30,45$\}$,
        rest type $\in\{$none,r1,r2$\}$) + charge duration $\tau_c$.
        Reward = negative incremental objective (identical to benchmark KPI).
  \item Teacher data: 772 deduped exact-tail LA runs, 36 families
        (3 route $\times$ 3 customer $\times$ 4 TW), 75{,}794 state--action
        pairs; per-stop states stored in solution JSONs (no replay needed);
        5 infeasible teacher runs excluded.  \todo{final counts after audit}
  \item DESIGN CHOICE (decided): the network outputs the FULL decision —
        discrete head (y $\times$ break $\times$ rest, masked softmax) +
        charge-duration head ($\tau_c$ as \todo{bins vs.\ regression — ablate}).
        NOT solver-in-the-loop duration recovery.  Reasons:
        (i) latency claim stays pure ($\sim$ms, no Gurobi at deployment);
        (ii) benchmark evidence: frozen-structure execution (2SP + duration
        recourse) strands 13.7\% of runs — value lives in per-stop adaptive
        decisions, which the student retains;
        (iii) safety via a worst-case reachability clamp on $\tau_c$
        ($e + \Delta e(\tau_c) \ge E_{\min}$ + buffer to next charger under
        support-max travel time) — same check every benchmark policy uses;
        clamp active $=$ logged event, report activation rate.
  \item Masking: infeasible discrete actions get $-\infty$ logits (same
        worst-case checks; e.g.\ must-rest when $sd$ at cap).
  \item Model + training (keep to $\sim$6 lines in final): MLP 2$\times$128
        ReLU; class-weighted cross-entropy (discrete) + \todo{L1/L2} on
        $\tau_c$; Adam; early stopping; inputs z-scored on train split.
        Splits BY INSTANCE SEED; entire families held out for OOD (below).
        5 training seeds, report spread.
  \item NOT in v1 (state as future work): DAgger, sequence encoder over
        remaining stops, RL fine-tuning.
\end{itemize}

\section{Discussion and outlook}\label{sec:disc}
\begin{itemize}
  \item General lesson for ML$\times$OR: measure WHERE learning can help before
        learning — here two classical axes (scenarios, horizon) were
        empirically dead; integrality under a latency budget was the live one.
  \item When exactness is batch-affordable but latency-prohibitive,
        distillation converts offline optimality into an online policy;
        certification against teacher + oracle is the honest scorecard.
  \item Limitations (say plainly): single vehicle; synthetic lognormal
        uncertainty; imitation $\le$ teacher by construction; preliminary
        scale.
  \item Next: DAgger (expert queryable at arbitrary states — solver, not
        human); predict-and-search variant (student proposes binaries, LP
        certifies) as the safety-critical deployment mode; fleet extension.
\end{itemize}
